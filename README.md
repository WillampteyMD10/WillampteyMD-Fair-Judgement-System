# WillampteyMD Fair Judgment System (SkepticOS)
### *"Quis custodiet ipsos custodes?" — Who Watches the Watchman?*

The **WillampteyMD Fair Judgment System** (commercially branded as **SkepticOS**) is an advanced enterprise cybersecurity governance and middleware framework. It acts as an objective referee layer sitting above detection systems, generative utilities, and human experts to dismantle the **"Toxic Triad"** of modern Security Operations Centers (SOCs).

---

## 🛑 The Core Problem: The Toxic Triad
When organizations stack multiple uncoordinated AI and human layers together, they create a dangerous, high-friction operational loop:
1. **The Worker AI (Generative AI):** Hallucinates fluid, plausible-sounding explanations and flattens out critical log anomalies to provide clean summaries.
2. **The Detective AI (Detection Rules):** Operates in a state of constant system paranoia, flooding human analysts with high-volume false-positive alerts.
3. **The Human Expert (The Analyst):** Becomes exhausted by the noise (**alert fatigue**) and begins blindly rubber-stamping AI conclusions (**automation bias**), rushing through tickets to clear the queue.
